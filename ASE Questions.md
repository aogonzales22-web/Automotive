# General Diagnosis

## Question 1
Which diagnostic tool connects to the Data Link Connector and allows the technician to read light duty diesel engine diagnostic trouble codes?

a. DMM (Digital Multimeter)

b. Scan tool or laptop computer

c. Engine Analyzer

d. GMM (Graphic Multimeter)

### Answer
B

Since OBD II (On-Board Diagnostic Level II), the industry has used a 5 character diagnostic trouble code that can only be read with another computer like a scan tool or PC based laptop computer. The other tools listed will not perform this function.


## Question 2
An orange colored liquid is found under the front of the engine, which of the following fluids is leaking?

a. Engine Oil

b. Coolant

c. ATF

d. Washer Fluid

### Answer
B

Coolant is orange, pink or green. Engine oil is yellow or brown. ATF is red. Washer fluid is blue.

## Question 3

A diesel engine exhibits low power and black exhaust smoke under load. A scan tool displays a diagnostic trouble code (DTC) for an underboost condition. Which diagnostic tool is MOST appropriate to check for a restriction in the exhaust system upstream of the turbocharger?

a. Exhaust backpressure gauge

b. Cylinder leakdown tester

c. Vacuum gauge

d. Fuel pressure gauge

  

### Answer

A

An exhaust backpressure gauge tapped into the exhaust manifold or turbine inlet measures restriction before the turbocharger. Excess restriction or low pressure can cause underboost and black smoke due to insufficient air mass.

  

## Question 4

A technician is using a scan tool to diagnose an engine that cranks but will not start. Desired rail pressure is 4,000 PSI, but Actual rail pressure reads 150 PSI during cranking. Which of the following is the MOST likely cause?

a. Faulty mass air flow sensor

b. Leaking injector return circuit or faulty fuel pressure regulator

c. Restricted exhaust pipe

d. Open glow plug circuit

  

### Answer

B

Common rail diesel engines require a minimum threshold pressure (typically 2,000–4,000 PSI depending on system) to enable injector pulsing. Excessive internal injector leakage returning to the tank or a stuck-open pressure regulator prevents pressure buildup during cranking.

  

## Question 5

Technician A says that freeze frame data stores a snapshot of engine operating conditions at the exact moment a diagnostic trouble code (DTC) sets. Technician B says freeze frame data automatically clears itself as soon as the ignition key is turned off. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

A

Tech A is right because OBD II freeze frame data captures sensor values at the moment a fault is detected. Tech B is wrong because freeze frame data remains stored in non-volatile memory until cleared by a scan tool or after a set number of trip cycles without the fault reoccurring.

  

## Question 6

An active diagnostic trouble code indicates a fault in the Intake Air Temperature (IAT) sensor circuit. When the sensor is disconnected, the scan tool live data reads -40°F (-40°C). What does this reading indicate?

a. The signal circuit is shorted to ground.

b. The sensor circuit is open.

c. The ECM is operating in closed loop.

d. The reference voltage is shorted to power.

  

### Answer

B

IAT sensors are negative temperature coefficient (NTC) thermistors where higher resistance equals lower temperature. Disconnecting the sensor creates an open circuit (infinite resistance), causing the ECM/PCM to display the minimum default reading of -40°F/°C.

  

## Question 7

Which of the following diagnostic tools is BEST suited to measure fuel injector current ramps and identify a dragging solenoid in an electronic unit injector?

a. Scan tool PID monitoring

b. Digital multimeter on max/min setting

c. Digital storage oscilloscope (lab scope) with a low-amp current probe

d. Logic probe

  

### Answer

C

A lab scope coupled with a low-amp current probe displays the current waveform (ramp) over milliseconds, allowing a technician to spot electrical shorts or mechanical binding in the injector solenoid coil.

  

## Question 8

Technician A says high fuel rail pressure during hot idle indicates a stuck-closed fuel pressure relief valve. Technician B says a high fuel rail pressure reading on the scan tool when the engine is key-on, engine-off (KOEO) indicates a failed rail pressure sensor or circuit fault. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

B

Tech B is right because rail pressure should drop to near 0 PSI when the engine is off; a high pressure reading at KOEO indicates an offset sensor or shorted signal wire. Tech A is wrong because a stuck-closed relief valve would not raise pressure at idle (it is normally closed); high pressure is usually caused by a faulty volume control valve or pressure regulator.

  

## Question 9

A scan tool indicates a "P0300 - Random/Multiple Cylinder Misfire" on a V8 light-duty diesel engine. What is the MOST likely non-electrical cause?

a. Low fuel pressure or aerated fuel

b. Faulty intake air temperature sensor

c. Blown head gasket between two cylinders

d. Restricted DPF

  

### Answer

A

Aerated fuel or insufficient low-pressure fuel delivery starves the high-pressure pump, affecting all fuel injectors randomly across the engine firing order.

  

## Question 10

When using a breakout box for electrical diagnosis on a diesel engine control unit (ECU), where is the breakout box installed?

a. Between the DLC and the scan tool

b. In series between the ECU and the vehicle wiring harness

c. In parallel across the vehicle battery terminals

d. Between the glow plug module and the glow plugs

  

### Answer

B

A breakout box connects inline between the ECU and its harness connector, providing pin test points to check voltages and signals without piercing wire insulation.

  

## Question 11

Technician A says clear, bubbles-free fuel flowing through a transparent sight glass in the low-pressure supply line indicates proper fuel delivery. Technician B says air bubbles in the sight glass during engine operation indicate a suction-side air leak in the fuel supply system. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

C

Tech A is right because clear fuel without aeration confirms no air ingress on the suction side. Tech B is right because air bubbles introduced before the lift pump or primary filter stem from vacuum leaks in supply lines, fittings, or fuel pickup tubes.

  

## Question 12

A light-duty diesel vehicle enters "Limp-Home" mode with reduced engine power. Which of the following is the MOST common cause for the ECM to command reduced power output?

a. Burned out tail light bulb

b. DPF soot load exceeding critical thresholds or extreme fuel rail pressure deviation

c. Low windshield washer fluid level

d. Weak glow plug on cylinder #1

  

### Answer

B

Engine control units enter derate/limp mode to prevent catastrophic mechanical failure or severe emissions violation when critical parameters (e.g., turbo overboost, extreme rail pressure error, or DPF overload) exceed limits.

  

## Question 13

Which measurement parameter on a scan tool confirms whether the ECM is actively attempting to correct an engine idle speed imbalance on individual cylinders?

a. Cylinder Fuel Balance / Injector Quantity Correction PIDs

b. Fuel Temperature Sensor Voltage

c. Throttle Position Sensor Percentage

d. Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) Duty Cycle

  

### Answer

A

Injector Quantity Correction (or balance rate) PIDs show the volume of fuel added or subtracted by the ECM for each cylinder to maintain smooth crankshaft speed.

  

## Question 14

A technician measures 0 Volts at the 5-volt reference (5V Ref) circuit at the Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) sensor with the key ON. Disconnecting which of the following sensors could help isolate a shorted 5V reference circuit if they share the same reference bus?

a. Fuel injector solenoid

b. Glow plug relay

c. Engine coolant temperature (ECT) or rail pressure sensor

d. Starter solenoid

  

### Answer

C

Sensors like the MAP, Rail Pressure, and Cam/Crank position sensors often share an internal 5V reference bus in the ECM. An internal short inside any one sensor pulls the entire 5V bus to ground (0V).

  

## Question 15

Technician A says oil dilution by diesel fuel can cause elevated oil level readings on the dipstick. Technician B says severe oil dilution in a diesel engine can lead to an engine runaway condition. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

C

Tech A is right because unburnt fuel passing by piston rings (or leaking internal injectors) accumulates in the oil pan, raising the oil level. Tech B is right because fuel-laced crankcase vapors pulled through the PCV/CDR system into the intake can act as an uncontrolled fuel source, causing diesel engine runaway.

  

## Question 16

What type of fault is indicated when a scan tool displays a DTC defined as "Circuit High Input" for a two-wire temperature sensor?

a. Short to ground in the signal circuit

b. Open circuit or short to voltage in the signal circuit

c. Sensor internally shorted to ground

d. Blown ECM main fuse

  

### Answer

B

Temperature sensors use thermistors supplied with a 5V reference. An open in the signal or ground wire stops current flow, causing signal voltage to stay at the high 5V pull-up reference voltage (interpreted by the ECM as Circuit High).

  

## Question 17

A technician needs to check for parasitic battery drain on a light-duty diesel truck equipped with dual batteries. How should the DMM be connected?

a. In parallel across the positive and negative terminals of one battery

b. In series with the negative battery cable after disconnecting the battery ground and isolating the second battery

c. In series with the starter main cable during engine cranking

d. In parallel across the alternator output terminal and ground

  

### Answer

B

Ammeters must be connected in series to measure current flow. On dual-battery diesel systems, auxiliary cables or secondary battery grounds must be disconnected/isolated so all drain passes through the meter connected inline on the main ground.

## Question 22

a. Faulty fuel rail pressure control valve (PCV) / metering unit

The fuel metering unit or rail pressure control valve regulates high-pressure pump output. Sticking or failing control valves cause erratic rail pressure delivery, leading to engine surge or idle hunting.

  

## Question 23

When conducting a relative compression test using a lab scope and current clamp, what signal is being measured?

a. Starter motor current draw during cranking

b. Alternator charging current at idle

c. Injector solenoid pull-in current

d. Glow plug current draw at key-on

  

### Answer

A

Relative compression measures starter motor current draw while disable-cranking the engine. Higher cylinder compression creates greater mechanical resistance, causing distinct starter current peaks for each cylinder.

  

## Question 24

a. A only

### Answer

## Question 25

A diesel engine exhibits low power without codes. When checking live data, the Boost Pressure Sensor reads 14.7 PSI at KOEO (sea level) and 30 PSI under full load. The Ambient Barometric Pressure (BARO) sensor reads 14.7 PSI. What do these readings indicate?

a. Boost sensor is shorted to ground.

b. The engine is producing 15.3 PSI of gauge boost pressure under load.

c. BARO sensor is reading incorrectly.

d. The turbocharger is producing 30 PSI of gauge boost pressure.

  

### Answer

B

Absolute pressure sensors read total pressure including atmospheric pressure (14.7 PSI at sea level). Absolute boost (30 PSI) minus atmospheric pressure (14.7 PSI) equals 15.3 PSI of true manifold gauge boost pressure ($30 - 14.7 = 15.3$).

## Question 27

Technician A says high fuel return volume from a common rail injector indicates severe internal needle or valve wear. Technician B says an injector with excessive return flow can cause a low-rail-pressure no-start condition. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

Tech A is right because internal valve wear allows high-pressure fuel to bypass directly into the return line. Tech B is right because excess return flow bleeds off rail pressure during cranking, preventing the system from reaching starting threshold pressure.

## Question 29

A technician diagnoses an engine misfire accompanied by excessive white exhaust smoke at cold start that clears up when warm. Which of the following is the MOST likely cause?

a. Inoperative glow plug on one cylinder

b. Clogged diesel particulate filter

c. Failed turbocharger wastegate

d. Leaking rear main seal

  

### Answer

A

Inoperative glow plugs cause poor combustion in cold cylinders, discharging unburnt raw diesel fuel as white smoke until combustion chamber temperatures rise naturally.

  

## Question 30

Technician A says measuring voltage drop across a battery cable test reveals high resistance under load. Technician B says a voltage drop reading of 0.8V across a starter ground cable during cranking is within acceptable limits. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

A

Tech A is right because voltage drop testing measures dynamic circuit resistance while current is actively flowing through the circuit. Tech B is wrong because acceptable voltage drop on a heavy ground cable during cranking is generally less than 0.2V to 0.5V; a drop of 0.8V indicates excessive resistance.

  

## Question 31

Technician A says a digital multimeter set to DC Millivolts can be used to measure voltage drop across a fuse to determine current flow. Technician B says a blown fuse will show 0 Volts when measuring across its two test terminals with the circuit turned ON. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

  

### Answer

A

Tech A is right because measuring small millivolt drops across the known resistance of an intact fuse indicates current flow without opening the circuit. Tech B is wrong because an open (blown) fuse under load acts as an open circuit, showing full source voltage across the open terminals when the circuit is activated.

  

## Question 32

A light-duty diesel engine has an active DTC for EGR Flow Insufficient. When performing a vacuum command test on the pneumatic EGR valve at idle, the engine stumbles and RPM drops. What does this test result indicate?

a. The EGR valve and passage are functioning mechanically.

b. The EGR cooler is completely plugged.

c. The EGR position sensor is shorted to ground.

d. The intake manifold is severely restricted.

  

### Answer

A

When exhaust gas is introduced at idle, it displaces oxygen and degrades combustion, causing engine stumble. A noticeable stumble proves the EGR valve opened and exhaust flow entered the intake manifold properly.
# Cylinder Head and Valve Train Diagnosis and Repair

## Question 1
Two technicians are discussing engine valve seals. Technician A says to apply a light coat of engine oil to new oil seals and push them in. Technician B says you can install the intake oil seal into the exhaust side or install the exhaust oil seal into the intake side because they are interchangeable. Who is right?

a. A only

b. B only

c. Both A and B

d. Neither A nor B

### Answer
A

Tech A is right because you need to apply a light coat of engine oil to new oil seals to help ease them in. Tech B is wrong because installing the intake oil seal into the exhaust side or installing the exhaust oil seal into the intake side can cause problems later because the exhaust side is generally hotter than the intake side and the seals are generally different and made of different material and therefore not interchangeable.
